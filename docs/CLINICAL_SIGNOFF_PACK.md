## 1. Outcome categories and priority hierarchy

Every valid screening ends in exactly **one** category. Priority order in code
(highest first): **any urgent flag → urgent; else any missing core field →
incomplete; else any moderate flag → further assessment; else routine.**
An urgent flag wins even when another measurement is missing.

| Category (code value) | Meaning | Trigger | Requires referral? | Suggested follow-up |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `urgent_escalation` | Urgent medical assessment | ≥ 1 flag with severity `urgent` | Yes | 1 day |
| `incomplete_information` | Information incomplete | No urgent flag, and ≥ 1 missing core field (§5) — or a validation error (§2, `isValid: false`, follow-up 0 days) | No | 2 days |
| `further_assessment_needed` | Medical assessment needed | No urgent flag, nothing missing, ≥ 1 flag with severity `moderate` | Yes | 7 days |
| `routine_follow_up` | Routine follow-up | No urgent/moderate flag, nothing missing | No | 30 days |

For every valid evaluation `requiresFollowUp` is `true`. Recommended next
steps attached per category:

| Category | Step 1 | Step 2 | Step 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| `urgent_escalation` | Generate urgent PHC referral immediately; arrange transport/accompaniment — **immediate, within 2–4 hours** | Keep patient resting; share screening summary with PHC Medical Officer — during transit | ASHA home follow-up to confirm PHC attendance — **within 24–48 hours** |
| `incomplete_information` | Obtain the missing measurements with calibrated instruments — **within 24–48 hours** | Do not reassure the patient that vitals are normal until measurements are complete — immediate guidance | — |
| `further_assessment_needed` | PHC referral for medical-officer assessment + confirmatory lab testing — **within 3–7 days** | Record a 7-day follow-up reminder to verify the PHC visit / treatment plan — **within 7 days** | — |
| `routine_follow_up` | Routine counselling (nutrition, hydration, salt/sugar moderation, symptom awareness) — during current visit | Schedule next routine screening — **in 30 days**, or earlier if new symptoms | — |

The result is presented in the app with the disclaimer (en): *"This result is
generated by a prototype rule-based screening module to help prioritize care.
It is NOT a clinical diagnosis or confirmed medical diagnosis. Urgent cases
must always be referred immediately to a qualified doctor."*

## 4. Blood pressure — rule order matters

Evaluated only when **both** systolic and diastolic are present. The branches
are an `if / else if` chain, evaluated **in this order** — the first matching
branch is the only BP flag produced:

| Order | Condition (observed BP) | Code | Severity | Rule ref. in code |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Systolic **≥ 180** OR diastolic **≥ 110** | `BP_URGENT_ELEVATION` | **urgent** | Prototype NPCDCS Triage Rule #BP-URG |
| 2 | Systolic **< 90** OR diastolic **< 60** (hypotension) | `BP_HYPOTENSION_ALERT` | **urgent** | Prototype Vitals Triage Rule #BP-LOW |
| 3 | **Pregnant** AND (systolic **≥ 140** OR diastolic **≥ 90**) | `MATERNAL_HYPERTENSION_URGENT` | **urgent** | Prototype Maternal Safety Rule #MAT-BP |
| 4 | Systolic **≥ 140** OR diastolic **≥ 90** | `BP_FURTHER_ASSESSMENT` | moderate | Prototype NPCDCS Screening Rule #BP-MOD |
| — | None of the above | *(no BP flag)* | — | — |

Consequences of the ordering, for the reviewer's attention: a reading such as
150/55 is flagged as **hypotension (urgent, branch 2)**, not hypertension,
because branch 2 is tested before branch 4; a pregnant patient at ≥180/110 is
flagged with the generic `BP_URGENT_ELEVATION` code, not the maternal code.

## 5. Missing fields — "missing is never normal"

| Core field | Missing means |
|---|---|
| `systolicBp` | BP not recorded |
| `diastolicBp` | BP not recorded |
| `temperatureC` | Temperature not recorded |
| `spo2Percent` | SpO2 not recorded |
| `gestationalWeeks` | Only required **when `isPregnant` is true** |

- Any missing core field adds one flag: `CORE_VITALS_MISSING`, severity
  `missing`, ref. *Prototype Data Completeness Safety Rule #SAFE-MISSING*,
  listing the missing fields.
- With no urgent flag, any missing core field makes the whole result
  `incomplete_information` (§1).
- **Not** treated as required by the engine: heart rate, respiratory rate,
  random glucose, haemoglobin, CBAC — their absence produces no missing-field
  flag and does not block a `routine_follow_up` result. Please confirm that is
  clinically acceptable, especially for haemoglobin in pregnancy (the engine
  does not require it).

## 10. Heart rate and respiratory rate

### Heart rate — one flag, severity depends on how far out of range

Flag is raised when heart rate is **> 120 bpm OR < 50 bpm** (exactly 120 and
exactly 50 raise no flag). Code: `HEART_RATE_ABNORMAL`, ref. *Prototype Vitals
Rule #HR-01*.

| Observed heart rate | Severity |
|---|---|
| **≥ 140 bpm**, or **< 45 bpm** | **urgent** |
| 121–139 bpm, or 45–49 bpm | moderate |

### Respiratory rate — urgent only, no moderate tier, no low-rate rule

| Condition | Code | Severity | Rule ref. in code |
|---|---|---|---|
| Respiratory rate **≥ 30 breaths/min** | `RESP_RATE_URGENT` | **urgent** | Prototype Respiratory Rule #RR-URG |
| Respiratory rate < 30 | *(no flag, at any lower value)* | — | — |

## 11. Symptoms

Symptom catalog in `src/lib/i18n.ts` (`SYMPTOM_CATALOG`). Each selected catalog
symptom marked `isRedFlag: true` produces its **own** urgent flag:
`RED_FLAG_<SYMPTOM_ID>`, severity `urgent`, ref. *Prototype Frontline Red-Flag
Escalation Rule #RF-01*.

### 11a. Red-flag symptoms (each → urgent)

| Symptom ID | Label (en) |
|---|---|
| `chest_pain` | Severe chest pain or tightness |
| `severe_breathlessness` | Severe shortness of breath at rest |
| `seizures_confusion` | Convulsions, confusion, or sudden weakness |
| `pregnancy_bleeding_vision` | Pregnancy danger sign: bleeding, blurred vision, or severe swelling |

### 11b. Non-red-flag catalog symptoms (no flag on their own — see 11c)

| Symptom ID | Label (en) |
|---|---|
| `persistent_cough_2w` | Persistent cough for more than 14 days |
| `high_fever` | Recurrent fever with chills or night sweats |
| `headache_dizziness` | Frequent headache or dizziness |
| `fatigue_pallor` | Marked fatigue or pale palms/conjunctiva |
| `excessive_thirst_urination` | Excessive thirst or frequent urination |
| `pedal_edema` | Swelling in feet or ankles |

### 11c. Symptom combination / duration rules (an `if / else if` — only one fires)

| Order | Condition | Code | Severity | Rule ref. in code |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `persistent_cough_2w` is selected, **OR** symptom duration **≥ 14 days** with ≥ 1 non-red-flag symptom present | `PROLONGED_SYMPTOMS_2W` | moderate | Prototype Respiratory/Infectious Rule #SYM-14D |
| 2 | Otherwise, **≥ 2 non-red-flag symptoms** selected | `MULTIPLE_SYMPTOMS_MODERATE` | moderate | Prototype Multi-Symptom Rule #SYM-MOD |

### 11d. Free-text / custom symptoms

| Input form | Treatment in code |
|---|---|
| Symptom ID starting `urgent:` | Treated as a red flag: `RED_FLAG_CUSTOM_URGENT`, **urgent**, ref. #RF-CUSTOM |
| Symptom ID starting `custom:`, or any ID not in the catalog | Treated as a **non-red-flag** symptom (counts toward 11c) |

Reviewer notes: in rule 11c/1, the flag's displayed duration is
`max(symptomDurationDays, 14)`, so selecting `persistent_cough_2w` alone
displays "14 days" even if a shorter duration was entered. A single
non-red-flag symptom with duration < 14 days produces no symptom flag at all.

## 12. CBAC — MoHFW Community Based Assessment Checklist NCD risk score

Implemented in `evaluateCbacScore`. The engine **sums scores supplied by the
caller** — it does not measure waist circumference etc. itself, and no numeric
waist cut-offs exist in the engine (see §13).

| Component (`CbacInput` field) | Score values, as commented in `src/lib/types.ts` |
|---|---|
| Age (`ageScore`) | 0 = < 30 yrs · 1 = 30–49 yrs · 2 = ≥ 50 yrs |
| Tobacco (`tobaccoScore`) | 0 = never · 1 = past / occasional · 2 = daily |
| Alcohol (`alcoholScore`) | 0 = no · 1 = daily / frequent |
| Waist (`waistScore`) | 0 = normal · 1 = high · 2 = very high *(numeric cut-offs not in the engine)* |
| Physical activity (`physicalActivityScore`) | 0 = ≥ 150 min/week · 1 = < 150 min/week |
| Family history (`familyHistoryScore`) | 0 = no · 2 = yes — BP / diabetes / heart disease |

| Result | Rule |
|---|---|
| Total score range | 0–10 (sum of the six components above) |
| **High NCD risk** | Total **> 4** (i.e. 5–10). Label: "Refer to Weekly PHC NCD Day" |
| Not high risk | Total **≤ 4** |
| No CBAC input supplied | `evaluateCbacScore` returns total 0 / not high risk; inside `evaluateScreening` CBAC is only evaluated when `cbacInput` is present |

Inside a screening, a high CBAC score adds one flag: `CBAC_NCD_HIGH_RISK`,
severity **moderate**, ref. *Prototype CPHC-NCD CBAC Rule #CBAC-04* — next step
per code: attend the weekly PHC NCD screening clinic for hypertension,
diabetes and cancer-prevention assessment. CBAC alone can therefore produce
`further_assessment_needed`, never `urgent_escalation`.

## 14. Reviewer questions / uncertainties recorded by the transcriber

These are questions about the code as written, **not** additional rules.
A signing clinician is asked to answer them in the relevant section's Notes:

1. **BP branch order (§4):** hypotension is tested before pregnancy
   hypertension and before general hypertension. Is an urgent hypotension flag
   the intended reading of e.g. 150/55?
2. **Haemoglobin (§9):** one threshold pair (< 7.0 / < 10.0 g/dL) for all ages,
   sexes and pregnancy. Are pregnancy- or sex-specific thresholds required?
3. **No low-side rules** exist for temperature (hypothermia) or respiratory
   rate, and no moderate tier exists for respiratory rate (§7, §10). Intended?
4. **Heart-rate band (§10):** the flag triggers at > 120 / < 50, but urgency
   only at ≥ 140 / < 45. Is the 121–139 / 45–49 "moderate" band intended?
5. **Optional vitals (§5):** heart rate, respiratory rate, glucose and
   haemoglobin may all be absent without the result becoming
   `incomplete_information`. Intended for every patient group, including
   pregnant patients?
6. **CBAC waist (§12):** the engine receives a pre-scored 0/1/2 waist score;
   the centimetre cut-offs behind "high" / "very high" are **not defined
   anywhere in the engine**, so they cannot be signed off from code alone —
   the reviewer should state the cut-offs to be used at data entry.
7. **Prolonged symptoms (§11c):** selecting `persistent_cough_2w` displays a
   duration of 14 days regardless of the duration actually entered. Display
   wording only — but should it be corrected?
8. **Age/sex in triage:** `evaluateScreening` receives `patientAgeYears` /
   `patientSex` but no rule in `PROTOTYPE-RULES-v1.0` uses them (pregnancy is
   the only demographic branch, via `isPregnant`). There are **no paediatric
   thresholds** — all thresholds are the same for a 2-year-old and an adult,
   although registration accepts ages 1–115. Is the engine to be restricted to
   adults until paediatric rules are specified?

## 15. Doctor Priority Queue — ordering heuristics (NOT triage rules)

The doctor portal's Priority Queue (`src/lib/priority-queue.ts`) orders
patients who **already** have an open need. It never assigns or changes a
screening category — §§1–13 remain the only triage rules. The ordering
factors below are **PROTOTYPE heuristics awaiting clinician review**;
the reviewer is asked to approve, adjust, or remove each one:

| Factor | Effect on queue order |
|---|---|
| Screening category | Base order: urgent_escalation ≫ further_assessment_needed ≫ routine_follow_up / incomplete_information. No other factor can lift a patient across a category gap. |
| Urgent / moderate flagged reasons | More (and more severe) flags from §§4–12 rank a patient higher within/across nearby scores. |
| Pregnancy | A pregnant patient is boosted within the queue. Ordering only — pregnancy triage itself is §4. |
| Age ≥ 65 years, or < 5 years | Boosted as "higher vulnerability". **These cut-offs are queue-ordering heuristics, not clinical thresholds** — note the tension with §14 Q8: the engine has no paediatric triage rules, yet under-5s are prioritised here. Reviewer to confirm or remove. |
| Waiting time | Longer wait since screening/referral raises rank (capped, so it breaks near-ties only). |
| Overdue follow-up | A patient whose follow-up is overdue is surfaced/boosted even if their screening category was routine. |
